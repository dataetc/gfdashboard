Contact Your CCM Representative

Country Coordinating Mechanisms (CCMs) serve as the coordination platform for Global Fund grants in each country. They bring together representatives from government, civil society, the private sector, and affected communities.

To find contact information for your CCM representative, please visit the CCM Dashboard.

The dashboard provides comprehensive information about CCM membership, structure, and contact details for each country.

Report Fraud and Abuse

The Global Fund takes all allegations of fraud, corruption, and abuse very seriously. If you suspect or have witnessed any fraudulent activities related to Global Fund programs, we encourage you to report it.

How to Report

You can report concerns through the Global Fund's I Speak Out Now platform, which allows for confidential and anonymous reporting.

  • Online: Visit the Office of Inspector General website
  • Email: ispeakoutnow@theglobalfund.org
  • Hotline: Available in multiple languages - check the OIG website for numbers

All reports are treated confidentially, and whistleblowers are protected from retaliation under Global Fund policies.

Not Sure Where to Start?

If you're unsure which option is right for you, here's a quick guide:

  • Request Support: Use this if you need technical assistance, have questions about implementation, or need guidance on Global Fund processes.
  • Contact Learning Hub: Best for regional resources, training opportunities, and connecting with other implementers in your region.
  • Contact CCM: Reach out to your CCM for country-specific coordination, funding request processes, or to raise concerns about grant implementation.
  • Report Fraud: Use this if you suspect corruption, misuse of funds, or other fraudulent activities.

Still not sure? Submit a request through our support form, and we'll help direct you to the right resources.
