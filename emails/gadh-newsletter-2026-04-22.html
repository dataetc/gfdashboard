Publications and resources

New advocacy tools from the GADH team and our partners. For more resources from the GADH, visit our resources page and our blog.
•  New U.S. government policies on health: What Global Fund advocates need to know about the PHFFA and what they can do. In January 2026, the U.S. finalized three rules that change the conditions under which organizations can receive foreign assistance. This brief explains what the PHFFA policy says, how it could affect the Global Fund and its recipients, and what community advocates can do right now. Read the blog post →
•  Wondering when your country might submit its Funding Request? Our CCM dashboard has our best estimates for each country's Technical Review Panel (TRP) window, estimated based on last cycle's window. Has your country's window changed? Let us know and we'll update it! Look up your country →
•  Grant Cycle 8 Allocation Letter Tracker. Our Allocation Letter tracker is now updated to include allocation amounts and transition status on every Global Fund-supported country. Find your country →
