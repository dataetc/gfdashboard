## What is the Eligibility List?

The Eligibility List is a Global Fund report that explains which country programs are eligible to receive Global Fund funding.  Country eligibility is specific to each disease and is calculated using a formula mostly based on country income category and disease burden.  While the list is published every year, this <a href='https://archive.theglobalfund.org/media/axqf0p1r/archive_eligible-countries-2026_list_en.pdf' target=_blank>2026 version</a> is important since it determines who is eligible for all of Grant Cycle 8 (GC8).

## If my country is Eligible, what does that mean for funding levels in GC8? 

The amount of funding available to countries has not yet been calculated. These amounts are likely to be published in March.  While eligibility is required to receive funding, being eligible does not guarantee that the country will receive an Allocation. This means that it is possible to be eligible and yet receive no funding in GC8.  

Unlike in past years, the Global Fund has published a new policy that blocks countries from receiving future funding after they have received Transition Funding and that allows the Secretariat to define accelerated transition timelines for country allocations in specific lower disease burden ‘middle’ income countries (MIC). 

## What does it mean if my country is eligible to receive Transition Funding?

After countries become ineligible in the Eligibility List, they are generally still eligible to receive one more three-year round of allocation funding.  The exception to this is if they are transitioning because they have been reclassified as High Income countries.
