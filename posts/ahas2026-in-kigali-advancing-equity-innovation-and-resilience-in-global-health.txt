The Africa Health Summit and Awards 2026 (AHAS2026) took place in Kigali, Rwanda, on 7-8 May 2026, bringing together leaders from government, civil society, academia, philanthropy, industry, and global health institutions under the theme “Advancing Equity, Innovation & Resilience in Global Health: Innovate, Integrate, Elevate: Reimagining Health Systems for Equity.” Convened by Zenith Global Health in partnership with the Rwanda Healthcare Federation, the summit served as a high-level platform for dialogue, reflection, and action on the future of African health systems. At the same time, the gala evening celebrated individuals and institutions whose work had advanced health equity, innovation, and accountability. The summit was officially opened by the Ministry of Health, Rwanda- Dr Oreste Tuganeyezu, Head of Clinical and Public Services.

Across the Kigali convening, the summit highlighted the urgent need to strengthen health systems through equity-centred innovation, resilient system strengthening, community-led and locally owned solutions, and stronger multisector partnerships through South-to-South learning. The programme focused on practical issues shaping the continent’s health future, including local production and pandemic preparedness, workforce development, innovation and emerging trends, health financing and governance, and health equity and the social determinants of health. These conversations reflected a shared determination to ensure that Africa not only responded to global health challenges but also shaped global health governance, financing, and priorities from a position of stronger sovereignty and leadership.

A major highlight of the Kigali gathering was the Africa Health Awards Gala on 8 May 2026, which honored transformative leadership, innovation, and impact across the health sector. The gala reflected the summit’s broader commitment not only to dialogue but also to recognizing those whose work is helping move health equity from aspiration to action, while inspiring the next generation of changemakers and future collaboration across the continent and beyond.

## GADH Spotlight

Recognized as the Outstanding Team of the Year at the Africa Health Awards and Summit, the Global Advocacy Data Hub (GADH) has emerged as a powerful civil society-led platform strengthening community voice and accountability in global health. Presented by Mr Nasser O. Okoth, Minister Counsellor at the Kenya High Commission in Rwanda, the award underscored GADH’s growing impact in equipping advocates with the data, evidence, resources, and peer support needed to influence national and global health decision-making. Built around three core pillars, organizing community advocates, creating pathways for community priorities to shape evidence-based advocacy, and strengthening advocacy through accessible data and tools, GADH was created in response to findings from the RISE study, which highlighted the urgent need for communities affected by HIV, tuberculosis, and malaria to be better represented in Global Fund decision-making spaces. Since its virtual launch in November 2025 and in-person launch at ICASA in December 2025, GADH has positioned itself as a vital platform for centering communities, promoting transparency, and ensuring that those most affected by health inequities are not left out of the decisions that shape their lives.
